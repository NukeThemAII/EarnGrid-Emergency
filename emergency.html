02

Direct strategy withdrawal

A last-resort path that bypasses EarnGrid entirely and talks to the underlying MetaMorpho vaults on Morpho Blue.

This is almost certainly not what you need. These buttons redeem your own shares in the MetaMorpho vaults. If you deposited through EarnGrid, you hold bvUSDC and these balances will read zero — that is correct, not a loss. EarnGrid's positions are owned by the vault contract, and only Section 1 can release them to you. This section only helps someone who deposited into a MetaMorpho vault directly.
For context, the row labelled EarnGrid vault holds shows where the protocol's assets currently sit. That balance belongs to all depositors collectively and is not withdrawable from here.
